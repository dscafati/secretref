# AI policy

AI help is welcome in this project. These rules keep review fair and history
honest.

- You own your change. You can explain every line of it in review.
- Disclose AI use in the Authorship section of the pull request.
- Do not add AI `Co-Authored-By` or `Claude-Session` trailers to commits. CI
  rejects them. See [decision 0012](docs/decisions/0012-no-ai-co-author-trailers.md).
- Keep at most 3 open pull requests at a time.
- Keep each pull request under 500 changed lines.

Maintainers may close pull requests that break these rules. For security
reports, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
